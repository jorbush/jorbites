This Cookies Policy explains how **Jorbites** ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar local storage technologies on our website, accessible at [https://jorbites.com](https://jorbites.com) (the "Website").

## 1. What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Local storage (such as HTML5 `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`) allows websites to store and retrieve data in your browser. They enable essential website functionality, remember preferences, and maintain secure sessions.

## 4. How to Manage Cookies and Browser Storage

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your web browser settings:

- **Chrome**: Settings -> Privacy and security -> Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox**: Options -> Privacy & Security -> Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari**: Preferences -> Privacy -> Manage Website Data

*Note: Disabling or clearing essential cookies will log you out of your Jorbites account.*
