# 🥖 Sandwiches of Canary Islands 🌴

📍 **Stage 17 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

Grand Finale in the sunny Canary Islands! Slow-roasted pata asada pork and zesty mojos bring vibrant island flavors to conclude our Tour of Spain on the highest note.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Canary Islands** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Canarian Pata Asada with Goats Cheese**: The reigning champion of Canarian taverns: juicy slow-roasted pork leg sliced paper-thin with mild island cheese and red mojo.
- **Carne Fiesta Marinated Pork Roll**: Pork cubes marinated in white wine, garlic, oregano, and paprika, flash-fried hot and crispy in crusty bread.
- **Island Tuna Steak with Green Cilantro Mojo**: Fresh seared tuna steak topped with fragrant green coriander mojo and quick-marinated red onions.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Canary Islands es bienvenida!*
