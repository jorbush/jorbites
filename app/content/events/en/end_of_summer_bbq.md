# 🔥 End of Summer BBQ 🍢

Say farewell to summer with a sizzle on Jorbites!

From August 22 to 31, fire up the grill and share your favorite barbecue dishes, grilled veggies, juicy skewers, and refreshing sides.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge
Publish a barbecue or grilled recipe during the event window to receive the End of Summer BBQ badge.

### 💡 Ideas to inspire you
- **Grilled Skewers**: Veggie, tofu, or marinated protein skewers.
- **Charred Corn on the Cob**: Topped with herbs and spices.
- **Grilled Peaches & Desserts**: Sweet summer fruits hot off the grill.
- **Cool Summer Salads**: Crisp pasta, grain, or garden salads.
