### 💡 Ideas & Inspiration for Your Leftovers

Need a spark of inspiration before tying your apron? Here are some classic leftover transformations:

- 🍗 **Leftover roast chicken or meat**: Crispy croquettes, shredded chicken tacos, pot pies, or savory empanadas.
- 🥘 **Stew or pot roast remnants**: Classic hash, bubble and squeak, shepherd's pie, or baked rice.
- 🍚 **Day-old cooked rice**: Golden egg fried rice, crispy rice patties, or warm fried rice bowls.
- 🥖 **Stale bread**: French toast, croutons, bread pudding, panzanella salad, or creamy salmorejo.
- 🥦 **Veggie trimmings & odd greens**: Rich homemade vegetable broth, velvety soups, frittatas, or stir-fries.
- 🧀 **Cheese rinds & scraps**: Rich four-cheese pasta sauces, quiches, or bubbling bakes.
