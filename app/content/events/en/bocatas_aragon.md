# 🥖 Sandwiches of Aragon 🏔️

📍 **Stage 6 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

From the snowy Pyrenean peaks to the hills of Teruel! Aragon brings hearty, comforting flavors and prime roasted meats that fit beautifully in warm rustic loaves.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Aragon** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Ternasco de Aragón Lamb Sandwich**: Tender strips of PGI milk-fed roasted lamb seared with sweet green peppers and a whisper of creamy garlic aioli.
- **Teruel DOP Cured Ham Baguette**: Thin shavings of Spain's first DOP mountain cured ham paired with ripe grated tomato and fragrant Bajo Aragón olive oil.
- **Graus Grilled Sausage Roll**: Prized spiced country sausage from Graus grilled over open flame and served with caramelized sweet onions.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Aragon es bienvenida!*
