# 🥖 Sandwiches of La Rioja 🍷

📍 **Stage 8 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

Welcome to Spain's wine capital and tapas haven! In Logroño and across La Rioja, sandwiches are served generously, packed with local cured meats and garden vegetables.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **La Rioja** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **La Zapatilla Riojana**: A giant slice of village bread rubbed with ripe tomato, topped with piping-hot seared cured ham and sweet paprika.
- **Picadillo riojano roll**: Tender spiced minced pork marinated with paprika and garlic, quick-sautéed in a smoking skillet into a warm crusty roll.
- **Laurel Street Mushroom & Prawn Toast**: A tribute to Logroño's Calle Laurel: stacked grilled mushrooms basted in garlic herb butter with a seared prawn on toast.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de La Rioja es bienvenida!*
