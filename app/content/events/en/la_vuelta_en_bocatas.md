# 🥖 Tour of Spain in Sandwiches

Welcome to Jorbites' grand gastronomic journey! We invite you to travel across Spain from coast to coast through one of its greatest culinary traditions: **the Spanish sandwich**.

Over the coming weeks, we will visit all **17 Autonomous Communities**, discovering how crusty artisan bread meets the finest regional culinary treasures.

---

## 🥪 How to Join the Challenge?

Taking part is simple, fun, and open to everyone! The route is organized into **two-week stages** (a new region unlocks every fortnight):

You have **two easy ways** to complete each stage and earn that region's official badge:

1. 🧑‍🍳 **Share your own recipe**: Publish a recipe on Jorbites for a traditional or creative sandwich from the active region.
2. 🍴 **Cook someone else's recipe**: Browse recipes from the active region, leave a comment with a photo of your dish, and check the **"I cooked this"** box.

Either option awards you the official stage badge on your Jorbites profile!
