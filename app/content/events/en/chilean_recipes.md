# 🇨🇱 Chilean Recipes 🇨🇱

Celebrate the vibrant and hearty cuisine of Chile on Jorbites!

On September 18 and 19, join the celebration of Chile's *Fiestas Patrias*! Whether you are gathering for a traditional *asado*, visiting a *fonda*, or cooking at home, this is your chance to share your favorite Chilean-inspired creations and explore the country's rich culinary traditions.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge

All Jorbiters who publish a Chilean-inspired recipe during the event window will receive the exclusive **Chilean Recipes** badge.

### 🥟 Recipe ideas to inspire you

- **Empanadas de Pino**: The quintessential Chilean baked empanada filled with seasoned beef, onions, hard-boiled egg, olives, and raisins.
- **Pastel de Choclo**: A comforting corn and meat pie with a subtly sweet golden corn topping.
- **Pebre & Sopaipillas**: A fresh cilantro, garlic, chili, and onion salsa served over crispy pumpkin flatbreads.
- **Anticuchos**: Flavorful skewers of grilled meats and vegetables, a festive *fonda* staple.
- **Cazuela**: A soul-warming traditional stew made with tender beef or chicken, corn on the cob, pumpkin, and potatoes.
- **Charquicán**: A rustic and hearty mash of pumpkin, potatoes, corn, and ground beef.
- **Mote con Huesillo**: A refreshing, iconic beverage made from cooked husked wheat and rehydrated dried peaches in spiced syrup.
- **Choripán con Pebre**: Grilled artisan chorizo served in crusty bread topped with fresh pebre.
- **Alfajores Chilenos**: Delicate pastries filled with luscious *manjar* (dulce de leche).

### 🌶️ What makes Chilean cuisine special

Chilean cuisine is shaped by its dramatic geography—from the Andes mountains to the Pacific coast and fertile valleys. It combines indigenous Mapuche ingredients like corn, potatoes, and *merquén* (smoked chili) with European culinary heritage, resulting in comforting dishes made for sharing with family and friends.
