# 🥖 Sandwiches of Galicia 🐙

📍 **Stage 12 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

Galician excellence! Renowned rustic Galician bread with its crackling golden crust and airy crumb provides the ultimate canvas for marinated meats and seafood.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Galicia** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Galician Zorza Pork Sandwich**: Tender minced pork marinated in sweet smoked paprika, garlic, and wild oregano, pan-fried into warm crusty Galician bread.
- **Lacón with melted Tetilla cheese & greens**: Slow-cooked cured pork shoulder layered with sautéed turnip greens and gooey melted DOP Tetilla cow's milk cheese.
- **Pulpo á Feira Octopus Roll**: Tender sliced boiled octopus drizzled with fruity olive oil, coarse sea salt, and a sprinkle of sweet and spicy pimentón.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Galicia es bienvenida!*
