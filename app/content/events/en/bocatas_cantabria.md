# 🥖 Sandwiches of Cantabria ⚓

📍 **Stage 10 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

Pure sea breezes and mountain valleys! In Cantabria, crispy fried calamari rabas are an essential ritual and Santoña anchovies are the black gold of the Bay of Biscay.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Cantabria** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Crispy Calamari Rabas Roll**: Fresh squid cut into tender strips, floured and flash-fried until shatteringly crisp, served with a squeeze of fresh lemon or garlic dip.
- **Santoña Anchovies with Cantabrian Cream Cheese**: Hand-cleaned premium Cantabrian anchovy fillets paired with mild, creamy local cow's milk cheese or artisanal mountain butter.
- **Braised Ink Squid Sandwich**: Baby squid simmered slowly in rich natural savory ink and sweet caramelized onions in a crusty baguette.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Cantabria es bienvenida!*
