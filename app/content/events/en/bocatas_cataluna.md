# 🥖 Sandwiches of Catalonia 🍅

📍 **Stage 5 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

Celebrating the world's most brilliant culinary invention: pa amb tomàquet. Rubbing a juicy ripe tomato and rich olive oil onto rustic bread elevates every single filling.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Catalonia** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Grilled Botifarra Pork Sausage Roll**: Authentic Catalan spiced pork sausage seared over the flames, served inside tomato-rubbed bread with creamy garlic alioli.
- **Gourmet Truffled Bikini**: The beloved Catalan toasted ham and melted cheese sandwich, pan-crisped with golden butter and elevated with black truffle.
- **Fuet de Vic on rustic bread**: Artisanal sliced cured pork fuet sausage layered generously over sourdough country bread rubbed with tomato and virgin olive oil.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Catalonia es bienvenida!*
