# 🥖 Sandwiches of Asturias 🍏

📍 **Stage 11 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

A natural and culinary haven! From village festivals serving fresh bollos preñaos to cozy cider houses and legendary mountain cheeses.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Principality of Asturias** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Bollu Preñáu (Chorizo-Baked Bread Roll)**: Soft, freshly baked bread roll concealing an Asturian cider chorizo that releases rich, smoky juices directly into the tender crumb.
- **Mini Cachopo Sandwich**: Crisp breaded veal cutlets stuffed with cured mountain ham and gooey melting cheese inside a rustic wood-fired roll.
- **Pork loin with Cabrales Blue Cheese cream**: Seared pork tenderloin smothered in a rich, warm cream sauce made from cave-aged Asturian Cabrales blue cheese.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Principality of Asturias es bienvenida!*
