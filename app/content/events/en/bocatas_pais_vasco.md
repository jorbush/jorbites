# 🥖 Sandwiches of Basque Country 🐟

📍 **Stage 9 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

Gastronomy at its finest! From the Cantabrian Sea to traditional cider houses, the Basque Country turns simple sandwich ingredients into culinary masterpieces.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Basque Country** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Salt Cod Cider-House Omelette**: The cider-house classic: a silky, runny egg omelette folded with flaked desalinated salt cod, sweet braised onions, and fresh parsley.
- **Bonito del Norte with Piparras & Anchovies**: Tender white tuna flakes preserved in olive oil, zesty pickled Ibarra chili peppers, and savory salted anchovies (the iconic Gilda in sandwich form).
- **Artisan Orio Txistorra roll**: Flame-grilled Basque sausage nestled inside a hearty rustic farmhouse baguette with blistered sweet green peppers.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Basque Country es bienvenida!*
