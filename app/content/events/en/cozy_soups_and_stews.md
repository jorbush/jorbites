# 🍲 Cozy Soups & Stews 🥣

Welcome the autumn crispness with comforting bowls on Jorbites!

From October 2 to 12, share your favorite homemade soup, stew, bisques, and broths designed to warm body and soul.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge
Publish a soup or stew recipe during October 2-12 to unlock the Cozy Soups & Stews badge.

### 💡 Ideas to inspire you
- **Creamy Butternut Squash Soup**: Smooth and garnished with pumpkin seeds.
- **Hearty Lentil Stew**: Nutrient-rich comfort food.
- **Mushroom Bisque**: Rich, velvety earthy flavor.
- **Minestrone**: Packed with autumn veggies and beans.
