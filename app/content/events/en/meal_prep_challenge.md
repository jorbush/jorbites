# 🥗 Meal Prep Challenge 🍱

Kickstart the autumn routine on Jorbites with smart batch cooking!

From September 3 to 14, showcase your organized meal prep containers, healthy grain bowls, make-ahead breakfasts, and balanced weekday lunches.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge
Publish a meal-prep friendly recipe during the challenge to unlock your Meal Prep Master badge.

### 💡 Ideas to inspire you
- **Quinoa & Roasted Veggie Bowls**: Easy to store and assemble.
- **Overnight Oats & Chia Puddings**: Grab-and-go morning energy.
- **Batch Cooked Stews & Curries**: Dishes that taste even better the next day.
- **Mason Jar Salads**: Layered salads that stay crisp all week.
