# 🥖 Sandwiches of Castile-La Mancha 🌾

📍 **Stage 15 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

In a village of La Mancha... bread and aged cheese are legend! The historic roadside inns immortalized by Cervantes still serve comforting slow-cooked treasures.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Castile-La Mancha** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Lomo de Orza Pork Confit & Manchego Cheese**: Tender spiced pork loin preserved in olive oil, served warm with shavings of cured DOP Manchego sheep's milk cheese.
- **Pisto Manchego with braised pork bites**: Slow-simmered medley of sweet peppers, tomatoes, and zucchini paired with tender sautéed pork cubes.
- **Shredded Pickled Game Meat Sandwich**: Braised partridge or venison preserved in an aromatic wine vinegar, garlic, bay leaf, and peppercorn marinade.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Castile-La Mancha es bienvenida!*
