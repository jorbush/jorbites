# 🥖 Sandwiches of Castile and León 🏰

📍 **Stage 13 of 17 — [Tour of Spain in Sandwiches](https://jorbites.com/events/la_vuelta_en_bocatas)**

The historic heartland of dense candeal bread, centuries-old roasts, Salamanca's rich hornazo pie, and sizzling Soria pork cracklings.

---

### 🥪 Iconic Local Sandwiches for Inspiration

Aquí tienes algunos de los bocadillos más emblemáticos de **Castile and León** para despertar tu apetito e imaginación:

- **Hornazo Salmantino**: A rich baked bread pastry layered with marinated Iberian pork loin, artisan chorizo, cured ham, and hard-boiled egg.
- **Soria Torrezno Crackling Baguette**: Thick-cut seared pork belly cracklings with a bubbly, shatteringly crisp crust and juicy meat in dense white bread.
- **Minute Steak Pepito with Aged Sheep Cheese**: Seared tender beef minute steak layered with shavings of aged raw sheep's milk cheese from Zamora.

*¿Tienes una versión familiar, un toque personal o una reinterpretación moderna? ¡Cualquier propuesta con el sabor de Castile and León es bienvenida!*
