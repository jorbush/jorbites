# 🍝 Pasta Perfection 🍅

Indulge in pasta heaven on Jorbites!

From October 15 to 28, show off your favorite pasta dishes—from hand-shaped gnocchi and tagliatelle to rich baked pastas and fresh pesto sauces.

### 🏆 Earn your exclusive badge
Publish a pasta recipe during the event window to earn the Pasta Perfection badge.

### 💡 Ideas to inspire you
- **Handmade Tagliatelle or Gnocchi**: Crafted from scratch.
- **Rich Mushroom & Walnut Sauce**: Earthy autumn flavors over pasta.
- **Classic Arrabbiata or Pesto**: Vibrant sauces that shine.
- **Baked Stuffed Shells**: Comfort food straight from the oven.
