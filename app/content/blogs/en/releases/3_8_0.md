# 🚀 Jorbites v3.8.0 is here!

We are thrilled to announce the release of Jorbites v3.8.0! This release brings major culinary enhancements with an interactive full-screen cooking guide, local progress persistence for ingredients and steps checklists, a unified minimalist design language across the entire platform, modernized blog pages with reader mode, list recipe card previews, and vital mobile and audio optimizations.

## 👨‍🍳 Interactive Cooking Experience

- 📖 **Interactive Full-Screen Cooking Guide**: Step through recipe instructions with an immersive, distraction-free cooking guide tailored for home cooks.
- ✅ **Interactive Checklist with Local Persistence**: Keep track of ingredients and steps while you cook; your checklist state is automatically saved locally so you never lose your place.
- ⚡ **Cooking Screen Stability**: Eliminated intermittent screen blinking when toggling and navigating the full-screen cooking guide.

## 🎨 Unified Minimalist Design System

- ✨ **Platform-Wide Aesthetic**: Harmonized the minimalist design language across recipes, workshops, community, events, leaderboard, and profile pages.
- 👤 **Profile UI Polish**: Modernized profile components, statistics, and preferences with responsive single-column layouts for smaller viewports.
- 🎟️ **Workshops & Events Directory**: Updated workshop listings and event directory with squircle styling and streamlined card layouts.
- 🌙 **Dark Theme Accuracy**: Fixed dark theme color mismatches across the culinary graph and badges.
- 📜 **Course Certificates**: Refreshed certificate view UI with direct top navigation sharing capabilities.

## 📰 Modernized Blog & Reader Mode

- 👓 **Reader Mode**: Enjoy distraction-free reading for blog articles, complete with optimized typography and layout.
- 💧 **Hydration & Cleanups**: Resolved hydration mismatches and removed redundant submission sections for a cleaner reading experience.

## 📋 Lists & SEO Enhancements

- 🖼️ **List Recipe Previews**: Recipe list cards now showcase quick recipe previews inspired by meal planning cards.
- 🔍 **SEO Improvements**: Enhanced metadata and structured markup across lists and list detail pages.
- 🟢 **Modal Polish**: Aligned save-to-list modals and checkbox styling with brand color palettes.

## 📱 Mobile PWA & Performance Fixes

- 🍏 **iOS PWA Safe-Area Insets**: Added proper support for iOS safe-area insets and reduced status-bar blur on the navbar and cooking guide in standalone mode.
- 🔊 **Web Audio on iOS Safari**: Unlocked Web Audio context interactions smoothly on iOS Safari.
- 🎬 **View Transitions**: Restored smooth ViewTransition morph animations during route navigation.
- 🧹 **Resource Cleanup**: Ensured proper revocation of Object URLs in image compression utilities and decomposed key components for better testability and performance.
