# 🚀 Jorbites v3.1.1 is here!

We are pleased to announce the release of Jorbites v3.1.1! This update focuses on fixing a pull-to-refresh timeout cleanup issue, rescheduling our upcoming cooking contest, and adding documentation for recent releases.

## 🛠️ Code Quality & Bug Fixes

- 🔄 **Pull Refresh Fix**: Resolved issues with pull refresh timeout cleanup, ensuring that timers and refresh states are cleaned up correctly when actions are completed or interrupted.

## 📅 Events & Community

- 🍔 **Burger Championship Rescheduled**: The highly anticipated second Jorbites Burger Championship (Smash Time!) has been rescheduled to **September 5, 2026**. Update your calendars!

## 📝 Documentation & Releases

- 📖 **v3.0.3 & v3.1.0 Documentation**: Added release blog posts for Jorbites v3.0.3 and v3.1.0 in English, Spanish, and Catalan.
